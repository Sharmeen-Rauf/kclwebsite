## How Complete Initial Data Prevents Filing Delays

Administrative holds during international flight permission processing most often occur when an initial submission omits an FIR boundary waypoint, Maximum Takeoff Weight (MTOW), or a current insurance certificate. Preparing a complete dispatch package allows Kashmir Corporation Pvt Ltd (KCL) to review your file and lodge the formal application with the aviation authority without back-and-forth delays.

## 1. Operator & Mission Identification

- Full legal name of the aircraft operator and billing address
- Three-letter ICAO designator and radiotelephony callsign
- Flight classification (**Scheduled** or **Non-Scheduled**) and specific operational purpose (e.g., commercial charter, technical fuel stop, ferry flight, cargo charter, air ambulance)

## 2. Aircraft Specifications

- Aircraft ICAO type designator (e.g., `B77W`, `A359`, `B744`, `GLF6`)
- Primary registration mark and any substitute tail numbers
- Maximum Takeoff Weight (MTOW) stated in kilograms

## 3. Complete Route String & UTC Schedule

- Departure and destination aerodrome four-letter ICAO codes
- Estimated Time of Departure (ETD) and Estimated Time of Arrival (ETA) in **UTC**
- Entry and exit waypoints for **Karachi FIR (`OPKR`)** and/or **Lahore FIR (`OPLR`)**, together with the complete ATS airway string across Pakistan airspace

## 4. Mandatory Aircraft & Operator Certificates

Depending on whether the request is an overflight, a technical stop, or a commercial traffic landing (needs verification per flight category), dispatchers should have current PDF copies ready of:

- Air Operator Certificate (AOC) and Operations Specifications
- Certificate of Registration and Certificate of Airworthiness
- Worldwide Third-Party Liability Insurance Certificate
- Noise Certificate and Radio Station License
- Crew and passenger count summary or cargo nature declaration (plus Dangerous Goods documentation where applicable)
