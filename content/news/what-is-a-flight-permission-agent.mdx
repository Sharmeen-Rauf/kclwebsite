## Why Airlines and Charter Operators Appoint a Local Permission Agent

Every commercial airline, cargo carrier, and charter operator crossing foreign airspace or landing at an international aerodrome must hold valid regulatory clearance prior to departure. In Pakistan, civil airspace is structured across the **Karachi Flight Information Region (`OPKR`)** and the **Lahore Flight Information Region (`OPLR`)**.

An **AFPA Authorized Flight Permission Agent** such as Kashmir Corporation Pvt Ltd (KCL) serves as the operator's accredited local coordinator. Rather than navigating local filing procedures and time-zone differences from abroad, an airline operations control center (OCC) transmits its schedule and aircraft documentation to KCL, and our 24/7 operations desk prepares, submits, and tracks the application.

## Core Coordination Responsibilities Handled for Dispatch Teams

- **Pre-Submission Route and Boundary Review:** Checking that planned ATS airways and `OPKR` / `OPLR` entry and exit waypoints align with published airspace procedures before the request is lodged.
- **Document Completeness Verification:** Reviewing the Air Operator Certificate (AOC), Certificate of Registration, Certificate of Airworthiness, Noise Certificate, and Liability Insurance so missing items are caught early.
- **Regulatory Liaison and Status Tracking:** Submitting seasonal schedule filings and non-scheduled charter applications through official authority channels and relaying any technical queries to the operator.
- **24/7 Amendment Coordination:** Submitting revision requests when weather delays, slot changes, or maintenance require an aircraft registration swap or an updated UTC departure window.

## Distinguishing Coordination from Regulatory Authority

A professional flight permission agent provides disciplined preparation and local follow-up, while final approval decisions, route conditions, and permit validity windows rest solely with the **Pakistan Civil Aviation Authority (PCAA)** and relevant government bodies. When a flight is landing at Karachi (`OPKC`), Lahore (`OPLA`), or Islamabad (`OPIS`), coordinating both the landing permission request and station handling through a single operations desk keeps the regulatory filing aligned with apron readiness.
